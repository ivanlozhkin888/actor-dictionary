    Build a portfolio to Korean standards

    Your portfolio is the most important document: without it agencies will not consider you. It has strict standards, and everything, from photos and video to cards, is made to Korean agency requirements: snaps, video intros, actor, model and skill cards. Agencies sign you based on your portfolio. No portfolio, no contract.

    My students build their portfolio with me, step by step. If you have never made one, I help at every stage: I break the work into small steps and explain each one. You rehearse at home before a photo or video shoot, and afterwards I check that everything is done right.

    Get into the databases of top agencies

    Hundreds of models and actors write to agencies’ general inboxes and never hear back. Why? A general inbox gets hundreds of emails, and yours is lost within a minute.

    My students write not to the general inbox but to the personal contacts of managers at the 250 biggest agencies, which I provide. The outreach takes about 2 weeks: emails go out gradually, to each agency separately.

    That is how you get into the databases of the largest South Korean agencies. At that moment Korea’s film and advertising industry learns there is a new actor or model, and it is you!

    Once you are in the databases, agencies send invitations: they want to sign you. Our job is to pick the biggest agency, one that works with Netflix, Disney+, LG, Samsung, BTS and other major brands.

The best visa for actors and models
E-6

A professional visa for actors, models and artists. With it you live in Korea and legally work in films, series and ads. In our program I help you get the E-6 visa.

Benefits

  • Work legally as an actor and model: films, K-dramas, ads, music videos
  • Live in Korea full time instead of leaving every 2–3 months
  • Issued for 1–2 years, then renewed
  • Your agency handles the renewal if it sees potential in you
  • You can move to a bigger agency and keep growing

How you get it

  1. We build a portfolio to Korean agency standards
  2. We sign with an agency that sponsors E-6 visas
  3. The agency applies through Korea’s Ministry of Culture
  4. We collect the documents: contract, portfolio, passport and more
  5. You get the visa: the whole process takes about 3 months
